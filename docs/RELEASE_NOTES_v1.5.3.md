# v1.5.3 — Colour-coded past-paper pills

A small visual upgrade that makes the Past Paper Library easier to scan at a
glance.

## Colour-coded metadata pills
Each past paper now shows colour-coded pills for its key details, so you can
tell paper number, tier and year apart without reading the label:

- **Paper number** — shades of one blue, stepped by the paper digit (Paper 1
  lightest, Paper 2 darker, and so on).
- **Tier** — shades of one violet (Foundation lighter, Higher darker).
- **Year** — a stable distinct colour per year, so papers from the same year
  share a colour and different years stand apart.

The pills sit alongside the existing Category pill and use inline colours, so
there's no change to how anything is stored or processed.
